# Project Process

## 1. Data Collection

The project consisted of multiple CSV datasets representing different areas of logistics operations, including customers, loads, trips, drivers, facilities, fuel, maintenance, and safety.

## 2. Data Preparation

The datasets were reviewed and prepared for analysis. Relevant fields were checked for appropriate data types, consistency, and usability within Power BI.

## 3. Data Modeling

A Power BI data model was created by connecting the operational tables and introducing a calendar table for time-based analysis.

The calendar covered January 1, 2022 through December 31, 2024.

## 4. DAX & KPI Development

Measures were created to calculate revenue, loads, average revenue, on-time performance, detention, fuel spending, maintenance costs, utilization, claims, incidents, and other operational KPIs.

## 5. Dashboard Development

Four dashboard pages were developed:

1. Revenue & Customers Overview
2. Delivery Performance & Detention
3. Fleet, Fuel & Maintenance
4. Driver Performance & Safety

## 6. Analysis

The dashboard was used to identify trends and operational issues related to delivery performance, detention, fuel costs, fleet utilization, maintenance, driver performance, and safety.

## 7. Recommendations

The final analysis was translated into practical recommendations focused on improving delivery performance, reducing detention, controlling fuel costs, optimizing maintenance, and improving safety.
